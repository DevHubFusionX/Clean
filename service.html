What is Integrated Facility Management (IFM)?

IFM is a method of consolidating all facility-related services under a single management team, improving efficiency and reducing costs.

How does Comtranz ensure service quality?

We employ skilled professionals and utilize proactive monitoring and regular site assessments to maintain high standards.

Can I customize my facility management package?

Yes, all our services are customizable to meet the specific requirements and budget of your residential or corporate property.

Do you offer emergency maintenance services?

We provide rapid response support for critical maintenance issues to ensure your facility remains safe and operational.

What sectors do you primarily serve?

We serve residential estates, corporate offices, schools, hospitals, and industrial facilities across Lagos and Nigeria.

How do you handle waste management?

We implement systematic collection and environmentally responsible disposal practices tailored to each facility's output.

Are your fumigation services health-safe?

Yes, we use professional-grade, safe chemicals and follow strict safety protocols to protect building occupants.

How often are site assessments performed?
