Raising Facility Management Standards in Nigeria

By : Admin Jan 28, 2026

Comtranz Global Facility Management is a Nigerian-based facility services company dedicated to providing world-class, integrated facility management solutions. We are driven by a vision to raise industry standards and improve the value, safety, and efficiency of buildings through proactive, affordable, and professional support.

“To be Nigeria’s most trusted and innovative provider of integrated facility management solutions, enabling sustainable and efficient environments.”

Our mission is to deliver exceptional facility management services through skilled manpower, innovative processes, and client-centric approaches that ensure optimal property performance and client satisfaction. We uphold core values of Integrity, Professionalism, Innovation, Reliability, and Customer Focus in every project we undertake.

From residential estates to corporate office buildings, we provide comprehensive services including facility maintenance, deep cleaning, landscaping, and security oversight. Our commitment to a preventive maintenance culture ensures that your assets are protected and operating at peak efficiency at all times.
